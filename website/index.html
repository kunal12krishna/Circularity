Four ways to open the wheel

drag+⇧ Shift

Tap Shift

While dragging from Finder, the desktop or any app. Option or Control work too.

shake your pointer here ↔

Shake

Wiggle left and right a few times mid-drag. Try it on the pad above.

⌃⌥C

⌃⌥C in Finder

Select files and press it. The wheel opens where your pointer is.

⌃⌥Space

The panel

Presets you can drop files on, recent results, and ⌘K: type 50kb or 1920w.

Physics break

Fling files into the wheel

Grab a file and throw it into the centre. Every hit converts it and adds to the bytes you've saved. Chain hits for a combo.

Themes, like the app

Pick a skin. Everything follows.

The app ships with themes and three wheel layouts. This page does the same trick: the wheel, the game and every button redraw in the style you choose. Secret: type shift anywhere.

What it doesn't touch

Small, quiet, private

0 permissions

No Accessibility, no Input Monitoring, no Screen Recording. macOS never asks.

12.5 MB at rest

Measured idle memory, 0.02 % CPU. It sleeps until you drag a file.

On your Mac

Apple's own ImageIO, PDFKit and WebKit do the work. Nothing is uploaded, ever.
